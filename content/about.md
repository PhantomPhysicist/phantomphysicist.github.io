I play badminton and table tennis very casually, and I cope by calling myself "decent". I cube, and I can solve the 2x2, 3x3, 4x4, and Pyraminx, and am learning to solve the Square-One.

I intend to learn GR and Quantum Mechanics on my own before the end of my first year. I'm working on a couple of other projects (which will probably be out before GTA 6), so stay tuned! Eventually, I will end up learning all these, and I do hope to write about them here.
