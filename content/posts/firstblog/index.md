### A Beginner's Guide to Minecraft

If you've seen the Minecraft Movie, you might know that creativity was key in helping the protagonists escape the sandbox back to the real world. Henry messed around with crafting recipes, engineering his own weapons against the many evils that came upon the group.

Unfortunately, you can't _craft_ whatever you want in Minecraft, so tater tot shooters aren't really an option. But Minecraft offers you a platform where you can do a lot of exciting stuff.

#### Building

Minecraft offers an incredible choice of colors and textures in the form of hundreds of in-game blocks. Similar to building in real life, you may first have to venture out and gather materials, but you can bypass that boring process by switching to creative mode. You can mess around with different building styles, from medieval-themed to modern, and make different builds. I myself built a medieval-looking Viking house with my friend once, and the fruits were incredible.

#### Redstone

"Redstone" is Minecraft's equivalent to electricity. Once again, it requires various materials to actually build with, but you can skip that using creative mode. Redstone allows you to get a taste for coding and circuitry, since, although slightly different, they all share the same core. You can use redstone to build yourself anything from a button-powered door to complicated machinery that can organize all your items into chests.

#### Have Fun!

Minecraft is a multiplayer game, allowing you to play with friends and family, no matter where in the world they are. You can build with your friends, explore the vast world, fight them in game, and so much more. Playing Minecraft is a really nice way to have fun with your friends. You can even vibe to in-game music together!

### THERE'S A BUT!
Make sure you stay safe. Don't trust random strangers with any of your info, and definitely don't spend all day playing Minecraft. In small doses, it's really fun and could even help you, but playing for too long takes a toll on you, both mentally and physically. Don't forget to touch grass :)
